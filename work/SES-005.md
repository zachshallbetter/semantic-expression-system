# SES-005 — Establish canonical vocabulary

Status: Completed — vocabulary defined

## Intent

Provide one normative vocabulary for SES semantics, expression, projection, operations, and conformance.

## Acceptance

- Core terms have definitions and non-equivalences.
- Operation existence, exposure, signaling, invocation, and outcome remain distinct.
- Morphology and Appearance remain distinct expression layers.
- Conformance and validation remain distinct.
- Vocabulary rules prohibit visual-language or renderer identities from entering the semantic model.

## Evidence

`docs/12-canonical-vocabulary.md`.

## Disposition

Accepted as a documentation-only vocabulary contract. Existing identifiers remain unchanged.
