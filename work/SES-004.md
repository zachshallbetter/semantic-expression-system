# SES-004 — Define the resolver boundary

Status: Completed — contract defined

## Intent

Specify the pure semantic-to-expression boundary that allows expression topology to vary without changing semantic identity or behavior.

## Acceptance

- Inputs and renderer-neutral outputs are explicit.
- Cascade order and protected semantic fields are explicit.
- Determinism, no-side-effects, resource ownership, and typed failure behavior are explicit.
- Renderer, business logic, transport, persistence, and operation invocation remain outside the boundary.

## Evidence

`docs/11-resolver-boundary.md` records the contract and invariants.

## Disposition

Accepted as a specification-only boundary. SES-010, SES-011, and SES-014 refine dependent portions without implementing the resolver.
