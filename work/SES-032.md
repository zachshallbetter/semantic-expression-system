# SES-032 — Verify accessible names, roles, and relationships

Status: Completed — accessibility metadata validation implemented

## Intent

Ensure expression output retains the accessible information needed for assistive technology and operation discovery.

## Acceptance

- Accessible names are non-empty.
- Roles are from the declared supported set.
- Relationship targets resolve to known semantic IDs.
- Required operations have stable IDs and accessible names.
- Missing or invalid accessibility metadata produces typed failures.

## Evidence

- `tools/accessibility_validation.py`
- `docs/conformance/accessibility-fixtures.json`
- `docs/15-accessibility-expression.md`

## Disposition

Accepted as metadata-level accessibility validation. Browser and assistive-technology execution remain later qualification work.
