# SES-037 — Measure resolver and rendering pipeline performance

Status: Completed — local performance measurement recorded

## Intent

Measure the current pure resolver and DOM/CSS adapter under a repeatable local method without converting one environment into a universal performance claim.

## Acceptance

- Resolver and renderer-adapter timings are measured separately.
- Sample count, units, environment, method, p50, and p95 are recorded.
- Resolver p95 is compared with the provisional 5 ms budget.
- A budget miss is reported as inconclusive rather than silently accepted.

## Evidence

- `tools/measure_pipeline.py`
- `docs/conformance/performance-fixture.json`
- `docs/22-performance-budgets.md`

## Disposition

Accepted as a local measurement. Cross-machine, production-load, and statistically independent benchmarks remain open.
