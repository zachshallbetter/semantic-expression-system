# SES-020 — Prepare protocol-neutral adoption guidance

Status: Completed — adoption boundary documented

## Intent

Describe how an application consumes SES while keeping transport, persistence, synchronization, authorization, and product navigation outside SES.

## Acceptance

- The host adapter, resolver, renderer adapter, and operation route are explicit.
- Transport, persistence, synchronization, authorization, and navigation have named owners outside SES.
- Adapter rules preserve semantic and operation identity and prohibit expression-driven business branching.
- Guidance supports partial or complete adoption without changing SES authority.
- No product implementation, external communication, deployment, or publication is required.

- `docs/23-authoring-boundaries.md`
- `docs/14-renderer-neutral-output.md`

## Disposition

Accepted as protocol-neutral adoption guidance. Framework-specific adapters and product integration remain host-owned follow-on work.
