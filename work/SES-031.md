# SES-031 — Verify operation exposure and event routing

Status: Completed — pure operation routing implemented

## Intent

Ensure required operations remain exposed and signaled, then route an event to a stable host-owned operation envelope without invoking side effects.

## Acceptance

- Required operation exposure and signaling are validated.
- Disabled or unexposed operations fail with typed diagnostics.
- Events route by stable operation identity and preserve source identity and payload.
- The router does not invoke callbacks, perform transport, or mutate state.

## Evidence

- `tools/operation_routing.py`
- `docs/conformance/operation-routing-fixtures.json`
- `docs/19-renderer-neutral-output.md`

## Disposition

Accepted as operation exposure and host-routing coverage. Host invocation and result handling remain application responsibilities.
